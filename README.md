### About me.

I'm a **Software Developer** working across **mobile** and **Quality Assurance**. I build cross-platform apps with **React Native**, shipping to both **iOS** and **Android** — from first commit to App Store and Google Play release. On the QA side, I write automated test scripts that cover critical functional flows and edge cases, and I design and optimize **CI/CD pipelines** so those tests run efficiently in production environments.

In my personal time, I build applications aimed at reducing service costs, leveraging the democratization of software development. I back these projects with my strong background in **security testing** and **vulnerability detection** to ensure they are safe and scalable.

### Projects

**[Sprite Catch](https://spritecatch.com)** · **100k+ downloads**
A free Fortnite Sprite tracker and checklist. Collectors tick off every Sprite and variant they own, see what finishing the season costs in Sprite Dust, and trade the rest with other players. Includes a trade board, community feed, leaderboards and a Discord bot — plus a camera scanner on iPhone. No ads, nothing purchasable.
React Native (iOS + Android) · web app · Discord bot

**[armatuMatch](https://armatumatch.com)**
The first app in Uruguay that connects padel players by skill level. Players find partners, book courts and split payments from their phone; clubs get smart bookings, analytics, CRM and integrated payments. Free for players, with tennis and pickleball coming next.
React Native (iOS + Android) · club manager portal · WhatsApp integration
